### About

Final-year B.Tech. student in Electronics and Communication Engineering at Manipal Institute of Technology. I work across the electronics stack, from transistor-level CMOS circuits and compact device models to interrupt-driven firmware on ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers.

- **Research:** transistor-level CMOS design and characterisation in the gpdk090 (90 nm) process, Department of ECE, MIT Manipal
- **Publications:** two peer-reviewed papers on CMOS logic design, at IEEE INSPIRE 2025 and as first author in a Scopus-indexed journal
- **Embedded:** STM32 firmware for real-time sensor acquisition (ADC, I²C) and event-driven control
- **Looking for:** opportunities in VLSI design, analog/mixed-signal circuits and embedded firmware
